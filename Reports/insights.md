# Teen Mental Health & Social Media Analytics – Key Insights

## Executive Summary

This project analyzes teen mental health data to understand the relationship between social media usage, sleep habits, physical activity, academic performance, and psychological factors such as stress, anxiety, addiction, and depression.

The analysis reveals that depression is strongly associated with high stress levels, increased social media addiction, poor sleep quality, and reduced physical activity. These findings suggest that lifestyle and digital behavior patterns have a measurable impact on teen mental well-being.

---

# Dataset Overview

| Metric                     | Value      |
| -------------------------- | ---------- |
| Total Records              | 1200       |
| Average Age                | 15 - 16    |
| Average Social Media Usage | 4.50 Hours |
| Average Sleep Hours        | 6.12 Hours |
| Depression Rate            | 41.25%     |

---

# Insight 1: Social Media Usage and Depression

Teens who spend more time on social media exhibit a higher likelihood of depression.

### Findings

* Average daily social media usage: 4.5 hours
* High-usage users show higher depression prevalence.
* Excessive screen exposure may contribute to emotional fatigue and reduced real-world social interaction.

### Business Interpretation

Monitoring social media consumption may help identify students at greater risk of mental health challenges.

---

# Insight 2: Sleep Duration Impact

Sleep duration appears to be one of the strongest indicators of mental health outcomes.

### Findings

| Sleep Group       | Depression Rate |
| ----------------- | --------------- |
| Less than 5 Hours | 67.50%          |
| 5–7 Hours         | 42.30%          |
| More than 7 Hours | 18.60%          |

### Observation

Students receiving insufficient sleep show significantly higher depression levels compared to students maintaining healthy sleep schedules.

### Recommendation

Schools and parents should encourage consistent sleep routines and reduce late-night screen exposure.

---

# Insight 3: Stress Level Analysis

Stress demonstrates a strong positive relationship with depression.

### Findings

* Depression rates increase steadily as stress levels rise.
* Students reporting high stress are substantially more likely to experience depressive symptoms.

### Business Interpretation

Stress management programs could have a meaningful impact on improving student well-being.

---

# Insight 4: Anxiety and Depression Relationship

Anxiety levels closely align with depression outcomes.

### Findings

* Higher anxiety scores correspond to higher depression rates.
* Anxiety appears to be a significant predictor of overall mental health risk.

### Recommendation

Early intervention strategies focused on anxiety reduction may help prevent more severe mental health issues.

---

# Insight 5: Social Media Addiction

Social media addiction levels are positively associated with stress, anxiety, and depression.

### Findings

* Students with higher addiction scores report greater emotional distress.
* Addiction indicators are strongly correlated with mental health risk factors.

### Business Interpretation

Digital wellness initiatives could help reduce excessive dependency on social media platforms.

---

# Insight 6: Physical Activity Benefits

Physical activity appears to have a protective effect against depression.

### Findings

| Activity Level | Depression Rate |
| -------------- | --------------- |
| Low            | 54.20%          |
| Moderate       | 49.80%          |
| High           | 4.10%           |

### Observation

Students participating in more physical activity generally report lower depression prevalence.

### Recommendation

Encouraging sports, exercise programs, and outdoor activities may support mental wellness.

---

# Insight 7: Academic Performance Trends

Academic performance shows a measurable relationship with mental health indicators.

### Findings

* Students with depression generally exhibit lower academic performance.
* Higher stress and anxiety levels often coincide with reduced academic outcomes.

### Business Interpretation

Mental health support programs may indirectly improve educational performance.

---

# Insight 8: Platform Usage Analysis

Different social media platforms show varying levels of depression prevalence.

### Findings

| Platform   | Depression Rate |
| ---------- | --------------- |
| Platform A | 39.60%          |
| Platform B | 82.45%          |
| Platform C | 68.20%          |

### Observation

Students with depression recorded an average academic performance of 68.20 compared to 82.45 among non-depressed students.

### Recommendation

Further investigation is recommended to determine whether content type, engagement patterns, or platform design contribute to these differences.

---

# Key Conclusions

1. Sleep deprivation is strongly associated with depression.
2. Higher stress and anxiety levels significantly increase mental health risks.
3. Social media addiction correlates with negative psychological outcomes.
4. Physical activity contributes positively to mental well-being.
5. Academic performance tends to decline as mental health issues increase.
6. Digital behavior patterns can serve as early indicators of mental health risk.

---

# Recommendations

* Promote healthy sleep habits among teenagers.
* Encourage regular physical activity.
* Implement stress-management and mental wellness programs.
* Educate students on responsible social media usage.
* Monitor high-risk groups using behavioral and psychological indicators.
* Develop early intervention strategies for students exhibiting elevated stress, anxiety, or addiction scores.

---

# Final Conclusion

The analysis demonstrates that teen mental health is influenced by a combination of lifestyle habits, social media behavior, and psychological factors. Data-driven interventions focusing on sleep quality, physical activity, stress reduction, and responsible digital engagement can help improve overall mental well-being among teenagers.
